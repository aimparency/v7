Purpose: keep the real economic objective visible after Felix truthfully selects

## The apparent contradiction

Felix wants funding to continue Aimparency, but does not yet claim the certainty
encoded by SPC's Founder Fellowship route: readiness to build a venture-scale
company and accept early financing. Selecting `Founder Fellowship` merely to
reach capital would weaken the application and obscure the actual decisions SPC
might help resolve.

`I'm exploring` does not abandon funding. It sequences conviction before a
specific financing commitment.

## SPC's stated paths

SPC says all applications begin together and it will help determine the path.
Its current public materials distinguish:

- **Founder Fellowship:** immediate membership and $1M total investment—$400K
  for 7% on a SAFE plus $600K guaranteed in the next external round—for founders
  ready to build a venture-scale company and accept funding;
- **Member Residency:** six months, no fee and no equity rights, for people
  exploring what to build, finding collaborators, and reaching conviction;
- **later investment:** SPC says it invests beyond the Fellowship, including at
  seed, and generally prefers to know founders as members first.

Residency is therefore not current funding and must never be represented as
such. It can be a path to higher-quality funding readiness if it resolves the
company, market, and founder questions.

## Scenario tree after application

### A. SPC discusses the Fellowship

Felix must decide personally whether he is ready for:

- the venture-scale company commitment;
- the SAFE and dilution;
- the in-person bootcamp and location;
- the founder/team representation;
- the opportunity cost of pursuing the direction-debt wedge full-time.

The application materials support diligence but cannot make this choice.

### B. SPC discusses Member Residency

Treat the six months as a conviction-and-evidence phase, not a waiting room:

1. run the direction-debt benchmark with qualified members;
2. discover whether the graph remains current outside Felix's own practice;
3. seek payment after a prevented failure;
4. resolve Robin's role or meet a better-matched collaborator;
5. decide whether the result is a venture-scale independent layer, an embedded
   developer feature, or a thesis to stop.

A later Fellowship or seed conversation should follow evidence, not elapsed
membership time.

### C. SPC invites an interview but no route is clear

Use the interview to ask what evidence separates Residency from Fellowship for
this case. Record the answer as an external decision criterion in the graph.
Do not interpret continued conversation as investment interest.

### D. SPC declines

The application still produces a coherent thesis and benchmark. Seek the
rejection reason if offered, then run the smallest external test without using
SPC acceptance as a proxy for customer evidence. A program decision is evidence
about founder/program fit; it is not by itself a verdict on direction debt.

## Evidence gates for financing readiness

These are proposed gates for Felix and Boardy to revise:

1. **Founder gate:** Felix chooses company-building as the primary path and can
   state what he is foregoing.
2. **Problem gate:** at least six of ten qualified teams show a recent costly
   direction handoff failure.
3. **Use gate:** at least two external teams maintain usable graph state without
   repeated study prompting.
4. **Outcome gate:** Aimparency beats the strong baseline on net direction time
   in most paired handoffs without a severe authority failure.
5. **Economic gate:** one team pays to continue or identifies a concrete budget
   and procurement path.
6. **Team gate:** Robin's role is explicit, or Felix deliberately proceeds solo
   or seeks a co-founder.

These do not determine valuation or prove venture scale. They make the choice to
raise less dependent on enthusiasm alone.

## Parallel economic logic

SPC is one funding attempt, not the entire economic strategy. The direction-debt
test should produce ordinary external evidence regardless of program route:

- paid repository implementation or stewardship if the developer wedge works;
- a narrowly scoped owner-led diagnostic only if it tests the same durable-intent
  invariant rather than becoming unrelated consulting;
- grants or programs where terms and purpose genuinely fit;
- conventional fundraising after founder and market conviction.

Do not count prospective investment, credits, unpaid pilots, or avoided personal
spending as revenue. Economic autonomy requires received money and actual costs.
