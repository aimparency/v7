# SPC selection-readiness audit

Basis: current first-party SPC framing plus the audited application packet.
This is not a prediction of admission and does not invent an evaluator rubric.
It asks whether the application currently answers the questions SPC's public
materials make salient: exceptional builder, frontier ambition, idea generation,
movement from -1 to 0, and readiness for the appropriate path.

## Summary verdict

**Technically strong and intellectually aligned; still vulnerable on causal
founder evidence and external necessity.**

The application no longer lacks a thesis, differentiation argument, or rigorous
learning plan. More autonomous prose will have diminishing returns. The largest
remaining improvements require Felix to supply lived incidents, commitment, and
relationship truth, and Boardy to challenge whether the problem feels urgent to
someone other than its builder.

## Highest-leverage human inputs

These are ordered by expected application improvement, not convenience.

1. **One direction-loss incident.** In five sentences: situation, intended
   objective, what context was lost, what wrong or delayed work followed, and
   what it cost. This turns direction debt from elegant vocabulary into earned
   knowledge.
2. **Founder causality confirmation.** Decide whether the move from writing code
   to directing AI work genuinely caused Aimparency, and name what existed in
   Felix's personal decision practice before the software.
3. **One contrasting artifact.** Link, context, constraints, Felix's exact
   contribution, and an observable standard of quality.
4. **Robin truth.** One unambiguous label: committed co-founder, prospective
   collaborator, prior collaborator, or absent; include permission to mention.
5. **SPC-specific leverage.** Name the people or interactions available through
   SPC that make the direction-debt benchmark or founder decision faster and
   better than working alone.

The conditional Airtable fields are operationally urgent but may not improve the
substance as much as the first two inputs.

## What not to spend more time on before those inputs

- additional names for the intent layer;
- more features or architecture detail;
- speculative market-size arithmetic;
- more recursive examples;
- autonomous-organization language;
- polishing three competing narrative variants;
- expanding the evidence library.

## Boardy success criterion

The call succeeds if Boardy causes at least one of these belief updates:

- direction debt is not the right painful problem;
- the user or handoff event must be narrower;
- the founder origin sounds retrospective and needs a different causal story;
- the proposed baseline or benchmark would not persuade a skeptical buyer;
- `I'm exploring` is correct but SPC is not uniquely valuable for the next test;
- the story is strong enough that remaining work should be fact capture, not
  strategic reframing.

Agreement without a sharper claim, objection, or disconfirming test is not a
useful outcome.

## Submission readiness rule

Move the application from `human-dependent` toward final review only when:

1. Airtable's exact conditional schema is captured;
2. the first two human inputs above are answered;
3. Robin and artifact representations are resolved;
4. Boardy's strongest objections and decisions are recorded;
5. all answers are assembled, character-checked, and link-checked;
6. Felix reads and authorizes every external representation.
