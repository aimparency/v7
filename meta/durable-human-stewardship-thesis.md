# Durable human stewardship in autonomous companies

The interesting future is not simply that an AI owns a company or that today's owner keeps absolute control forever. It is that an institution becomes a durable human–AI machine: able to operate beyond any participant's working life while remembering who built it, why it exists, whom it serves, and how new contributors can earn standing.

Pre-AI work matters because it created the relationships, capital, craft, legitimacy, data, and institutions on which autonomous production will depend. Erasing those claims overnight would be both unjust and destabilizing. But converting them into unlimited hereditary control would freeze accidents of today's ownership into permanent rent extraction. Aimparency should explore a middle form: durable stewardship with explicit obligations.

## The constitutional bargain

An autonomous company has an inspectable constitution represented by aims, constraints, and decision rights. A founder's durable claim is not merely “I was here first.” It is a bundle:

- economic participation for value already contributed;
- stewardship authority while defined duties are fulfilled;
- the right to nominate succession, subject to constitutional checks;
- protection of the mission against opportunistic capture;
- an obligation to preserve the company, its people, and its legitimate stakeholders.

Some rights can outlive the founder through a trust, foundation, cooperative, family vehicle, or other jurisdiction-specific structure. They should not all be perpetual. Operational authority should decay or become reviewable when stewardship duties are not performed. Economic rights can be bounded by time, return, or share of surplus. Mission-protection rights can persist longer but remain challengeable through a legitimate process.

## How the machine remains alive

The graph is the constitutional memory, not the sovereign. It records:

- the mission and its history;
- current owners of decisions and their scope;
- claims earned by capital, labor, relationships, knowledge, or risk;
- evidence that an office-holder still performs their stewardship duty;
- succession rules and emergency powers;
- mechanisms by which workers, customers, communities, and future builders earn influence.

Agents can continuously propose and execute actions, but they cannot silently rewrite the constitutional layer. Material changes require the consent rule attached to that part of the graph. This makes autonomy compatible with continuity without pretending that an old intent can answer every future question.

## The anti-feudal test

A durable claim is legitimate only while it does at least one of these things:

1. rewards a real prior contribution under a known bargain;
2. finances continued risk or maintenance;
3. protects a mission or stakeholder group that would otherwise be vulnerable;
4. supplies accountable judgment the autonomous system cannot legitimately make.

If a claim only extracts value and blocks adaptation, the constitution needs a buyout, sunset, dilution, appeal, or removal mechanism. New contributors must have a visible path to earn both economics and voice. Otherwise “remembering the builders” becomes hereditary enclosure rather than cooperation.

## Aimparency's near-term experiment

Every transformation pilot should end with a one-page owner constitution:

- mission and non-negotiable boundaries;
- decisions delegated to the loop;
- decisions reserved for named humans or roles;
- who benefits from gains and bears failures;
- evidence that keeps delegated authority active;
- pause, rollback, appeal, and succession rules.

The experiment is not whether this document can predict the distant future. It is whether an owner will delegate a real workflow because the arrangement feels economically useful, inspectable, and legitimate—and whether workers and collaborators consider the bargain acceptable. Repeated pilots can reveal which constitutional primitives deserve to become product features.

The public thesis is therefore: **AI can make companies operationally immortal, but only legitimate stewardship can make that continuity worth preserving.** Aimparency's role is to make that stewardship explicit, revisable, and executable.
