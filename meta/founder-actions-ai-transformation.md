## Discovery call

Ask only enough to qualify a paid diagnostic:

1. Which recurring workflow currently consumes disproportionate owner or team attention?
2. What event starts it, and what observable result ends it?
3. What does one run cost in time, delay, mistakes, or missed revenue?
4. Which decisions must remain human, and who owns them?
5. What evidence could establish a before/after result?
6. Would solving the diagnostic itself be worth paying for, even if automation is not recommended?

Do not promise autonomy on the call. The next offer is a fixed-scope diagnostic with a price, output, schedule, access boundary, and stop condition.

## Speaker proposal

**Title:** From isolated AI tools to an owner-governed autonomous company

**Abstract (100 words):**

Most companies are collecting AI tools while leaving their operating system unchanged. Aimparency takes a different route: start with the owner's mission, map one economically meaningful workflow, make decision rights and stop conditions explicit, and automate only what can be measured and reversed. In this live diagnostic, we turn a real workflow into an inspectable graph of goals, dependencies, evidence, and human authority. The audience sees where agents can act, where people must decide, and how to test whether the change creates value after compute and supervision costs. The goal is not fewer humans. It is a company that can act continuously without losing legitimate human direction.

Audience takeaways:

- A method for selecting the first workflow by value, reversibility, and evidence.
- A practical boundary between agent autonomy and owner authority.
- A survival test based on real costs, outcomes, and willingness to pay.
