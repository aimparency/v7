# Operator validation sprint: 23 July–6 August 2026

## Decision

Pause product expansion that is not demanded by a qualified operator workflow.
For two weeks, test whether Aimparency can earn access to real operations and a
paid or clearly funded experiment.

The first two conversations are Wilde Möhre and Herzog Records. The remaining
eight should be owner-led organizations where Felix or a trusted introducer can
reach a person who operates the workflow, not merely an AI enthusiast.

## Falsifiable result

The sprint succeeds only if at least one organization:

1. reconstructs a real recurring workflow with us;
2. permits access to the evidence needed to establish its baseline;
3. names the decision-maker and human-only boundaries;
4. commits operator time; and
5. agrees to pay for, or identifies committed third-party funding for, a
   bounded diagnostic or pilot.

Meetings, compliments, introductions, hypothetical interest, feature requests,
and an unpaid open-ended proof of concept do not satisfy the result.

## Sequence

1. Felix sends one low-friction invitation from
   `founder-actions-ai-transformation.md`.
2. Reconstruct the latest real workflow run before showing Aimparency.
3. Quantify frequency, failure modes, human dependency, and economic cost.
4. Ask directly about access, operator time, decision authority, budget, and
   reasons to reject the experiment.
5. Record the result the same day in
   `operator-validation-sprint-2026-07.csv`.
6. Offer the fixed-scope diagnostic only when the evidence supports it.
7. After five calls, update the offer or target segment from observed patterns.
8. After ten calls, decide: continue the wedge, narrow it, replace it, or stop.

## Build gate

During the sprint, new implementation must satisfy at least one condition:

- a qualified operator needs it to run the bounded experiment;
- it is necessary to capture authoritative before/after evidence;
- it materially reduces delivery time or cost across more than one prospect;
- it fixes a defect blocking the current product from being shown or used.

A2A exploration remains a peer architecture pressure test until a customer
workflow requires interoperable specialist agents. It must not delay the first
operator conversation.

## Evidence review

At five and ten conversations, calculate:

- invitations → conversations;
- conversations → real workflow access;
- workflow access → funded diagnostic;
- median estimated workflow cost;
- repeated failure modes;
- repeated objections;
- percentage of proposed delivery that is reusable product capability;
- expected delivery margin including founder time, model cost, integration,
  and support.

Preserve negative evidence. If operators will not expose the workflow, invest
time, or fund a bounded diagnosis, change the wedge before expanding the
product.
