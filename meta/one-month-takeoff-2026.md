# Aimparency one-month takeoff

Window: 23 July–23 August 2026

## The bet

AI capability may increase by orders of magnitude while compute expands from
terrestrial datacenters toward vastly larger energy and space infrastructure.
If intelligence and execution become abundant, the scarce layer is not another
generic agent. It is:

- legitimate, durable intent;
- ownership and decision authority;
- access to companies, institutions, capital, and physical systems;
- trustworthy evidence about what actually happened;
- the ability to revise strategy without losing the mission.

Aimparency can become that layer: the inspectable strategic will of a
human–AI organization, coordinating increasingly capable specialist agents
without surrendering purpose or pretending that activity equals value.

## Two inseparable engines

### Survival engine

Obtain one paid or clearly funded owner-led workflow experiment. Measure:

- access to the real process;
- owner and operator time;
- baseline cost or failure;
- price and payment;
- model, integration, supervision, and founder cost;
- external result and willingness to continue.

The current first route is Wilde Möhre, followed by Herzog Records and eight
additional operator conversations. Grant and angel work can extend runway, but
does not replace customer evidence.

### Moonshot engine

Demonstrate one continuous, inspectable chain:

1. a human states the company mission and reserved decisions;
2. Aimparency models the strategy and chooses a bounded next bet;
3. a specialist agent receives minimized context and explicit authority;
4. the specialist returns an artifact and provenance;
5. Aimparency verifies the result against external evidence and real cost;
6. the graph updates its belief and chooses what to attempt next;
7. the human can inspect, redirect, or stop the system.

This is the seed of an autonomous company and the A2A strategy-agent vision.
The demo should operate on the same workflow and evidence machinery as the
first customer experiment. A separate science-fiction prototype is a failure.

## Allocation

Until a qualified operator conversation exists:

- 60% customer access, offer, evidence, and funding;
- 30% moonshot narrative and the smallest end-to-end proof;
- 10% defects that block use or make the graph misleading.

After a customer exposes a workflow, implementation follows that workflow. Do
not use the allocation to postpone a real call.

## Four weekly gates

### Week 1: contact and clarity

- Send Wilde Möhre and Herzog Records invitations.
- Reach five additional operators through warm paths.
- Produce a one-sentence category and a three-minute moonshot walkthrough.
- Record objections rather than polishing around them.

### Week 2: access and selection

- Complete at least five operator conversations.
- Select one real workflow only if access, operator time, authority, and budget
  are plausible.
- Freeze the bounded outcome, baseline, stop conditions, and human-only gates.
- Decide whether the moonshot demo can use this workflow.

### Week 3: deliver and expose

- Run the paid/funded diagnostic or smallest authorized experiment.
- Preserve the external-value run record.
- Show the continuous mission→delegation→evidence→strategy loop.
- Publish no client detail without explicit permission.

### Week 4: conversion and signal

- Ask for payment, continuation, referral, or explicit rejection.
- Calculate net economics including founder time.
- Turn the proof into one talk/demo for owners, funders, and agent builders.
- Choose the next month from evidence: deepen, narrow, change wedge, or stop.

## Takeoff scoreboard

By 23 August:

- 10 operator conversations;
- at least 2 real workflow reconstructions;
- at least 1 paid or committed-funded experiment;
- 1 externally evidenced run record;
- 1 three-minute moonshot demo using the same core system;
- at least 1 credible public or private presentation;
- a dated decision based on actual revenue, cost, access, and external outcome.

The month fails honestly if these do not happen. A larger graph, more agent
integrations, simulated income, internal praise, or an impressive unrequested
demo do not substitute for them.
