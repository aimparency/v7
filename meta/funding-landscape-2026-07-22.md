official source confirms that it is open now or gives a credible future window,
and when Aimparency has a plausible applicant structure.

### 3. NLnet regular open call — prepare, do not submit yet

**Fit: potentially high for the open protocol. Action now: monitor reopening.**

NLnet says its general calls are temporarily paused during the transition from
NGI Zero to Open Internet Stack and should reopen after summer 2026. The only
current calls, due 1 August, concern GNU Taler and Fediversity; Aimparency does
not fit them and should not force a proposal. Recheck the official apply page in

### 4. EXIST Gründungsstipendium — conditional founder route

**Fit: medium if personal and institutional eligibility hold. Action: one
eligibility conversation before writing an application.**

EXIST funds up to three founders for twelve months through a German university
or research institution. Current published support is €3,000/month for a
founder with a doctorate, €2,500 for a university graduate, €2,000 with a
vocational qualification, or €1,000 for a student, plus material and coaching
budgets. At least one team member needs a university degree; graduate timing,
employment, prior incorporation, and a willing university network can be
decisive. Do not spend time drafting until Felix and Robin's exact education,
graduation dates, employment, and company status pass the official EGS check
with a university startup network.

### 5. Pro FIT Berlin — conditional on a Berlin company

**Fit: medium/high for a Berlin technology company; otherwise ineligible.**

Pro FIT early-stage financing supports a newly founded small technology company
based in Berlin. Phase 1 can finance eligible expenditure 50% as grant and 50%
as loan, with total early-stage financing up to €500,000 and Phase 1 capped at
€200,000. The company-age limit is normally 12 months for Phase 1. This route
conflicts with a Bavarian registered seat and should not drive incorporation
without a broader location decision. Ask IBB for a scoping call before adapting
the existing draft.

### 6. BayTOU — conditional on a Bavarian company

**Fit: plausible but unconfirmed. Action: advisory eligibility call after the
location decision.**

BayTOU supports technology-oriented formations and young companies in Bavaria.
It may fit a Hohenwart-based Aimparency company, but the current official page
does not expose enough current conditions to justify a budget or application
claim here. Verify applicant age, eligible development work, own contribution,
and start-before-approval rules with the project sponsor before drafting.

### 7. ZIM — monitor only; applications paused

**Fit: technically plausible after incorporation, unavailable now.**

ZIM supports company R&D and ordinarily funds small-company individual projects
at up to 40%, or 45% for a small young company or one in a structurally weak
region. However, all ordinary project applications and sketches have been
temporarily stopped since 7 July 2026 because of budget demand. International
partner calls are the stated exception. Keep the technical draft, but do not
present ZIM as a current funding path or spend founder time submitting until
the official stop is lifted.

## What not to prioritize

- The current NLnet Taler and Fediversity calls: wrong technical scope.
- ZIM submission while the official stop remains: impossible.
- Pro FIT without a Berlin company, or BayTOU without a Bavarian company.
- EXIST before checking founder/university eligibility.
- Large EU consortium or EIC applications before customer evidence, a settled
  legal entity, and a credible co-financing/runway story.
- Digitalization vouchers intended to buy third-party adoption services: they
  do not normally finance building the vendor's own core product.
