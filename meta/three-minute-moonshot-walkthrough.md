# Three-minute survival-to-moonshot walkthrough

## One-sentence category

Aimparency is the inspectable strategy and intent layer through which owners and
AI agents can turn a company mission into bounded work, verify real outcomes,
and continuously improve what the company does next.

## Truth labels

- **Working now:** aim graph, priorities, human gates, MCP access, agent loop,
  evidence/reflection records.
- **Being tested this month:** one paid owner-led workflow and its real
  economics.
- **Longer-term direction:** Aimparency as the strategy agent coordinating
  independently operated specialist agents, including through A2A.

Never collapse these labels into one claim.

## Walkthrough

### 0:00–0:25 — The problem

**Screen:** A small owner-led company at the center of a simple workflow sketch.

**Voice:**

> Most companies are adding AI tools without changing how purpose and authority
> travel through the organization. The expensive part is often one recurring
> workflow that lives in a person's head. If that person is absent, the process
> slows down—and a more capable AI does not automatically know what it is
> allowed to optimize.

### 0:25–0:55 — Start with a real economic wedge

**Screen:** The operator-validation aim, bounded workflow, baseline, price,
human-only decisions, and stop conditions.

**Voice:**

> Aimparency starts much smaller than “make the company autonomous.” We map one
> costly, chaotic, or person-dependent workflow. The owner defines the result,
> the evidence, the decisions that remain human, and the point at which the
> experiment stops. This month we are testing whether an owner will expose a
> real process, invest team time, and fund a bounded experiment.

### 0:55–1:30 — Show the strategic graph

**Screen:** Move from the owner mission through the one-month takeoff aim to a
bounded child aim. Toggle priority coloring: high aims gold, low aims dark blue.

**Voice:**

> The mission is not hidden in a system prompt. It is an inspectable graph. Value
> flows from the mission into strategies and tasks; cost flows back. Connections
> explain why work matters. The loop can return to the largest mission, select a
> bounded next step, and still show the owner exactly what it is pursuing and
> why.

### 1:30–2:00 — Delegate without surrendering the company

**Screen:** A bounded delegation card: outcome, minimized context, budget,
authority, success evidence, and cancellation.

**Voice:**

> A specialist agent should receive only the outcome, context, budget, and
> authority it needs. It does not receive the whole company or the right to
> reinterpret its purpose. Aimparency can use MCP to work with its own graph. In
> the longer-term A2A direction, it becomes the strategy agent in a network of
> independent specialists.

### 2:00–2:30 — Reality returns to the graph

**Screen:** External-value run record: verification, beneficiary response,
model cost, founder time, revenue, net value, and belief update.

**Voice:**

> Agent activity is not success. The result comes back with provenance and is
> checked against the authoritative system. We record model cost, integration,
> supervision, founder time, payment, and the beneficiary's acceptance or
> rejection. That evidence updates the strategy. If the workflow loses money or
> nobody wants it, the graph must learn that.

### 2:30–2:55 — The moonshot

**Screen:** Zoom from one company graph to a network of specialist agents and
then back to the human mission.

**Voice:**

> Now imagine intelligence becoming a hundred times more capable and compute
> expanding far beyond today's datacenters. Execution becomes abundant. The
> scarce layer is legitimate intent, ownership, authority, institutional access,
> and trustworthy evidence. Aimparency's moonshot is to become that strategic
> layer for human–AI companies: continuous action without losing the mission.

### 2:55–3:00 — The ask

Choose one audience-specific ending:

- **Owner:** “Give us one recurring workflow and thirty minutes to determine
  whether a bounded paid diagnosis is worthwhile.”
- **Funder:** “Fund the month in which we connect this working strategic layer
  to its first externally valued outcome.”
- **Agent builder:** “Pressure-test one concrete strategy-agent exchange with
  named inputs, authority, evidence, and a date.”

## Evidence required before recording

- Replace any hypothetical workflow with a real one only after permission.
- Show no private aim, contact, client, credential, or financial data.
- Do not say A2A is implemented; say the boundary is specified.
- Do not say Aimparency has revenue until payment or a signed commitment exists.
- Preserve the final run record and external response alongside the recording.
